Findings fixed during T4:
1. With thinking on, reasoning tokens count inside `max_tokens`: a 20-token cap returned empty content. Caps raised (parse 1,500, phrase 600) with a doubled retry; `make deepseek-check` OK.
2. Phrasing call switched to thinking off (D19): same verified output, 3–5 s and hundreds of tokens saved per answer; DeepSeek timeout raised to 30 s after one 21 s outlier at effort low.
3. The model dropped "windows of at least 1 hour" from a listing header: the verifier now requires the zone label and every qualifier (D20); the phrase prompt names them explicitly.
4. Host build now copies migrations, prompts and static files into `dist/` (`scripts/copy-assets.mjs`), so `make ask` works outside Docker.

`make ask` (CLI sandbox) exercised live with eight questions in both languages, including an alias and a zone override; all correct.
