Rows 16:00/17:00 (optional overlap pairs) were not created by the owner; observed free as expected for an empty slot. Conclusion: Google free/busy semantics behave exactly as assumed in scope §7 (D2 verified).

Two findings fixed during T3:
1. Interval arithmetic mixed the busy intervals' UTC zone with the schedule zone, so a window boundary borrowed from a busy block formatted in UTC ("1:30 PM" for 9:30 New York). Every instant is now carried in the display zone and the renderer formats in that zone explicitly.
2. Cal.com clips busy-times at `dateTo` (the all-day block on the 11th came back cut at 10:00 local when `dateTo` was the 11th) and also returns the day before `dateFrom`. The engine already widens by a day on each side; the live test now fetches through the engine's path. Documented in `docs/architecture/availability.md`.
